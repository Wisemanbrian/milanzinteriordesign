ABOUT SAMANTHA

Samantha Heaven Milanzi is a multi-award-winning Malawian interior designer and the founder of Milanzi Interiors. With over 8 years of industry experience, she is known for her eclectic, sustainable, and soulful approach to design.

Since founding Milanzi Interiors in 2020, Samantha has led the studio with a fearless creative vision, delivering high-quality interior solutions with refined execution and attention to detail.
