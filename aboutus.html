THE COMPANY

OUR STORY

WE create LUXURY INTERIORS that STAND the TEST of TIME

Our interiors embody comfort, sophistication, and practicality. Milanzi Interiors is a premium interior design studio operating in Malawi and South Africa, specialising in bespoke residential and commercial interiors for private clients and developers across the region and beyond.

OUR STORY

Milanzi Interiors is a Multi award-winning Malawian interior design firm known for its quality craftsmanship and renowned for its eclectic, sustainable, and soulful approach to design. Reputable for capturing the heart and soul of every project in masterful design to detail.

Founded in 2020 by Samantha Heaven Milanzi, Milanzi Interiors the full service Turnkey firm has pushed the boundaries of interior design trends, establishing itself as a fearless leader in the creation of truly one-of-a-kind spaces.

OUR VISION & PHILOSOPHY

Design is more than aesthetics. It’s purpose, emotion, and experience.

We fuse functionality, originality, and meaning to create spaces that tell stories. Every project is an opportunity to craft an environment that not only looks beautiful but enhances the way you live, work, and feel. Our approach honors both commercial and residential needs, always with an eye for detail and a commitment to excellence.
